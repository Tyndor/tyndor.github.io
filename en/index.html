Office Fauna

The satirical tracker for your meetings

Office Fauna measures how much of your working time goes into meetings and phone calls, and whether that time was well spent. Every conversation gets a rating on a five-step scale, and 59 behavior patterns can be identified through three questions about observed behavior; every pattern comes with suggestions for handling it. All of this is wrapped in a field guide in which a conversation is an expedition and the rating a weather report from sunny to thunderstorm.

Stapelhaus

Who lives where? Your building at a glance

Work in progress

Stapelhaus keeps track of who lives where in a building: floors as a stack of soft building blocks, a floor plan seen from above for each floor, and the people who live in each apartment. For anyone who wants to stay on top of things in a larger building.
