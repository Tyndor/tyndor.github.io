This site is a text-only site with no account, no sign-in and no forms. It sets no cookies, embeds no fonts, maps, videos or analytics tools from third-party servers, and loads nothing further. Everything you see here comes from the same server as the page itself.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. or GitHub B.V. When you access it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical connection data in order to deliver the page and to protect the service against attacks and abuse; according to its own statements, GitHub logs and stores the IP address of every visitor for security purposes. We ourselves have no access to these logs and do not analyze them.

The legal basis for our use of GitHub Pages and the associated transmission of your connection data to GitHub is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our interest in a secure and reliably reachable publication of this site. GitHub itself is responsible for its own processing, such as its security logging.

Data may be transferred to the USA and other third countries in the process; GitHub relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses for this. How long GitHub retains this data depends on the purpose and on GitHub's legal obligations. Further details in GitHub's privacy statement and in GitHub's note on data collection for GitHub Pages.

4. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

No analytics or advertising services run on this site; there is no audience measurement and no profiling. The advertising referred to in the app's privacy policy concerns the app only, not this site.

6. Your rights

Subject to the statutory requirements, you have in particular the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. Insofar as we process personal data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you have the right to object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

You do not have to give us any personal data to use this site; there is no account and no registration. Contacting us by email is voluntary as well.

To exercise your rights, you can contact us at any time (contact details above). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular with our competent supervisory authority, the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony (Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen), or with the supervisory authority at your place of residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.
